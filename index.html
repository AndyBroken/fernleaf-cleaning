FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call or text us — a real person answers.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Nope. Most clients give us a door code or leave a key. Your cleaner texts you when they arrive and when they're done, with photos.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes — we bring everything, including vacuums and plant-based, pet-safe products. If you prefer your own products, just let us know.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back to re-clean the areas you're not happy with, free of charge. That's our Happiness Guarantee.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Of course. Reschedule or cancel for free up to 48 hours before your appointment by phone, text or email.

Are your cleaners employees?

Yes. Every Fernleaf cleaner is a background-checked, trained and insured employee — never a random contractor.
